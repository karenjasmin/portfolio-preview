Project Overview

This project focused on improving existing content for an industrial equipment rental website. My work involved reviewing pages and articles, making on-page SEO updates, and using Google Search Console to understand search visibility.

The SEO Challenge

Technical industrial topics need clear, useful content that addresses what people are searching for. Existing pages also benefit from descriptive metadata and relevant internal links that make related information easier to discover.

My Approach & Implementation

I reviewed search performance in Google Search Console, worked on page titles and descriptions, refined existing content, and added contextual internal links where relevant. These tasks focused on practical, page-level improvements rather than a full website redesign.

SEO Work Examples

Project Outcome

Completed on-page SEO work across existing content, including metadata adjustments, content refinements, and internal linking. Google Search Console was used to review performance; specific traffic, ranking, or lead improvements are not claimed without verified comparative data.
