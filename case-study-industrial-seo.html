The SEO Challenge

The project focused on improving the on-page optimization of existing service pages for an industrial equipment rental website. The work involved identifying relevant search terms, refining page content and metadata, and strengthening internal links to better align the pages with their target keywords and search intent.

My Approach & Implementation

I conducted keyword research to identify relevant search terms for industrial equipment rental services. Using WordPress, I updated existing page content, refined metadata, and added internal links to support keyword relevance and site navigation. I also used Google Search Console to monitor search performance and evaluate changes over time.

SEO Work Examples

Project Outcome

Completed on-page SEO updates across existing service and resource pages, including keyword research, content refinement, metadata optimization, and internal linking. The work established a more consistent approach to optimizing page content for relevant search queries, with ongoing performance monitoring through Google Search Console.
