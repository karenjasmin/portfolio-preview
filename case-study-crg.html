WORDPRESS · ELEMENTOR · RESPONSIVE PAGE DESIGN

CRG | Service Area Pages & Elementor Template

Creating a Service Areas directory and a reusable city landing-page layout for Coastal Resource Group's existing WordPress website.

Project
Coastal Resource Group (CRG)
Platform
WordPress · Elementor
My contribution
Page assembly, layout customization, responsive adjustments
Deliverables
Service Areas directory and Houston city-page template

Project Overview

CRG needed a clear way for visitors to browse its service locations and explore location-specific information. I worked within its existing WordPress website to build a Service Areas directory and an Elementor layout for the Houston, Texas service page.

The Project Challenge

The pages needed to organize service-area information, industrial products, industries served, and frequently asked questions without becoming difficult to navigate. The Houston page also needed a layout that could be adapted for other locations.

My Approach & Implementation

I assembled and customized sections in Elementor, including the Service Areas directory, Houston hero section, product and industry grids, service information, benefits, and an interactive FAQ accordion. I adjusted the layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile views, and incorporated review feedback and revisions.

The work focused on implementing and refining individual pages within an existing website, rather than building the entire CRG website.

Project Screenshots

Project Outcome

Delivered a Service Areas directory and a reusable Houston city landing page template using WordPress and Elementor. The layouts organize product categories, industries, service information, and FAQs into consistent sections that can be adapted for additional locations.
